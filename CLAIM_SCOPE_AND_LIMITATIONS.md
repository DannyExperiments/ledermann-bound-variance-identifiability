# Claim scope and limitations

For every pair p = m(m + 1)/2 and r = p − m with m ≥ 4, the theorem concerns the unrestricted real covariance model with strictly positive diagonal variances. Uniqueness and ambiguity each hold on a nonempty open set. The triangular formulation compares loading matrices up to column signs.

The ambiguity proof guarantees at least two admissible decompositions in its open region. Exact fiber counts and the generic complex degree remain outside the theorem. The small-noise bound uses a separately specified local injectivity radius and supplies no algorithm for computing one. The finite replay checks explicit witnesses and the figure. The all-dimensions assertion follows from the proof. External peer review and formalization are pending.
