# Mathematical audit

The PSD rank chart gives tangent directions annihilating the kernel block. At equality the dimension equals the number of off-diagonal coordinates. The two regularity determinants are basis independent and nonzero for a simple explicit kernel frame. Local projection from pairs of orthogonal positive and negative planes proves the simultaneous regularity step.

The two Gram forms differ by an invertible diagonal, and positivity and rank follow from their block forms. Their inverse-function neighborhoods have overlapping off-diagonal images. The strict diagonal inequalities produce a nonempty open ambiguity region. The kernel-projector test controls every competing PSD completion in a small-noise neighborhood. Rank continuity and local injectivity then exclude any second rank-at-most-r competitor in that neighborhood.

The fixed triangular parametrization is a Cholesky chart modulo column signs. Both branches remain in the chart, and exact support, if required, follows by restricting to an open dense subset of each branch. The [exact checks](../../verification/src/replay.py) corroborate two finite cases.
