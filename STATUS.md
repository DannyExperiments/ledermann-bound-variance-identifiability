The paper answers the remaining generic global variance identification question at Ledermann equality. In every remaining pair a nonempty open set exhibits multiple admissible positive variance diagonals, and another nonempty open set exhibits uniqueness.

The argument has undergone internal mathematical review. External peer review and formalization are pending. The [literature audit](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md) distinguishes earlier local and algebraic results from the real global statement.
